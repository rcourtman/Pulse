Authenticode-signed and may display an Unknown Publisher warning. This is the
version-bound owner exception recorded in
`v6.3.0-unsigned-windows-owner-approval-2026-08-22.md`; exact-SHA builds,
SHA-256 checksums, detached signatures, immutable-manifest validation, and
published-digest verification remained mandatory and passed. Stable `v6.3.1`
and later require Authenticode unless a new explicit version-bound exception
is recorded.
